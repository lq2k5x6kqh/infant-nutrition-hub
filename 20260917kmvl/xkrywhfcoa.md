# 【援外人物】梅立：我在佛得角，为患艾妇女做手术

> 更新时间：2026-09-17 (UTC+8)

**“I do operations for AIDS-suffering women in Cape Verde”——An interview with Mei Li, National Role Model in Foreign Aid Medical Services**

 文 《大众健康报》记者 蒲晓莉

**【人物名片】**

梅立，男，49 岁，九三学社社员，1992 年同济医科大学医学专业毕业，到四川省人民医院参加工作至今。主要擅长宫颈疾病及宫、腹腔镜的手术治疗，现任四川省人民医院妇产科生殖内分泌专业组长。中国援佛得角第17 批医疗队队员，并任 2016 年 -2018 年援佛得角医疗队队长。

**【Introduction】**

Mei Li, male, 49 years old, a member of the Jiusan Society. In 1992, he graduated in Medicine from Tongji Medical University and has worked in Sichuan Provincial People’s Hospital ever since. Mei’s forte is in cervical diseases and uteroscopic and laparoscopic surgeries. Currently, he is the head of reproductive endocrinology team of the Obstetrics and Gynecology Department of Sichuan Provincial People’s Hospital. He was one of China’s 17th batch of medical team members to Cape Verde, and served as head of the medical team to Cape Verde in 2016-2018.

美丽的佛得角

“这不仅是我个人的荣誉，也是整个医疗队的荣誉。”每当谈起获得“全国援外医疗工作先进个人”称号时，梅立总是谦虚地表示这份荣誉属于大家。“This is not only a honor for me, but also for our entire medical team.” Whenever asked about the title of “National Role Model in Foreign Aid Medical Services”, Mei Li modestly responded by saying that this honor belongs to everyone.

**两年援外，此生不可替代的人生历程**

**Two-year foreign aid medical service is an irreplaceable experience in my life**

2016 年，47 岁的梅立确认参加 2016 年 -2018 年援佛得角医疗队时，家人有些顾虑，“不了解非洲，那里的气候、疾病都是未知数”。In 2016 when 47-year-old Mei made up his mind to go to Cape Verde with Chinese foreign aid medical team from 2016 to 2018, his family had some misgivings about his decision, thinking that “Mei knows little  about Africa where he may face climate and disease uncertainties”.

为了让家人放心，梅立查阅了许多关于佛得角的资料。了解到佛得角位于北大西洋的佛得角群岛，虽然资源匮乏，但由于人口少、政治稳定、旅游业发展蓬勃，生活质量位居非洲各国前列。梅立把自己了解到的信息一一转述给家人，他想让家人更了解自己即将生活和工作的地方。To reassure his family, Mei consulted a lot of information about Cape Verde. He learned that this country is located on the Cape Verde islands in the North Atlantic Ocean. Despite scarcity of natural resources, few countries in Africa can rival it in terms of quality of life due to a small population, political stability and booming tourism. Mei told his family all of this, wanting them to know more about where he was going to live and work.

梅立与产妇合影

打消了家人的顾虑。2016 年11 月，梅立正式踏上了援外医疗的征程，经过 30 多个小时的飞行，他和队员到达了受援国——佛得角，在普拉亚中心医院这个他们即将工作两年的地方落了脚。Thus, he managed to dispel his family’s misgivings. In November 2016, Mei embarked on the journey of foreign aid medical service. After a more-than-30-hour flight, he and his fellows arrived in Cape Verde. For the two years to come, the medical team was going to work at a local hospital, the Central Hospital of Praia.援医两年间，梅立带领全队队员完成门诊6000多人次，收治住院 病人8000多人， 手 术7000多人次，接生2000多人次，B超1500多人次，抢救200多人次，麻醉1500多人次，参加当地学术活动 30 余次。Over the past two years, his medical team completed more than 6,000 outpatient cases, more than 8,000 inpatient cases, more than 7,000 surgery cases, more than 2,000 delivery cases, more than 1,500 ultrasound scans, more than 200 emergency cases and more than 1,500 anesthesia cases, and attended more than 30 local academic symposiums.援医团队的辛劳付出，受到当地医生和患者的一致认可。Hence, local doctors and patients have spoken highly of the hard work of the medical team.

佛得角旅游业发展蓬勃

**带病工作，为20多位患艾产妇做手术**

**Mei did operations for 20-odd pregnant AIDS sufferers in spite of hypertension**

初到普拉亚中心医院，队员们对医院的基本情况进行了了解。“普拉亚中心医院算得上佛得角最好的医院，但由于医疗资源匮乏，医院的医疗水平仅相当于国内的县级医院。”梅立打了个比方，简单介绍了佛得角的医疗条件。这样的医疗条件，让队员们对今后两年的援外工作有了些许顾虑。Upon their arrival at the Central Hospital of Praia, the team members tried to familiarize themselves with the basic information of the hospital. “In a sense,the Central Hospital of Praia is the best in Cape Verde. But due to inadequate medical resources, its medical level is only equivalent to that of domestic county-level ones,” Mei explained briefly the medical conditions in Cape Verde by analogy with domestic hospitals. That caused some concerns among the medical team about working here in the coming two years.面对现状，只能迎难而上。在国内，梅立是四川省人民医院妇产科生殖内分泌专业组长，在国外，他既是一般的住院医师，也是主任医师。当其他医生遇到无法开展的手术时，梅立就要“自动”转变为主任医师，完成一些难度较高的手术。But what they can do then was do their best to rise above whatever difficulties they would face. At home, Mei is the head of reproductive endocrinology team of the Obstetrics and Gynecology Department of Sichuan Provincial People’s Hospital. While in Cape Verde, he was both a general resident and chief physician. Whenever his team members had difficulty in doing some operations, Mei would “automatically” double as chief physician to complete some relatively complicated operations.

梅立与患者合影

佛得角的生育率高，产科急诊多，梅立还要和其他医生 24 小时轮流值班。他还记得“有时值班，会做 10 多个剖宫产手术，一天只能休息很少的时间”。如此高强度的工作，即便是身体健康的人也吃不消，而梅立还是一位高血压患者，由于长期作息不规律，他的血压一直得不到较好地控制。Due to a high birth rate, there were a great number of obstetric emergencies in Cape Verde. As a result, the medical team members and Mei alike had to work 24-hour shifts in turn. “Sometimes, I did up to over 10 caesarean sections during one shift, which means I had little time for rest,” recalled Mei. Even for people in good health, such a workload was very heavy, let alone for Mei who suffered from hypertension. Due to a long-term irregular lifestyle, his blood pressure was abnormal all along.除了工作强度大外，他在手术中也会面临健康威胁。一次，梅立为一名艾滋病患者做剖宫产手术，即便万分小心，在手术中还是被器械刺伤。“被刺伤的那一刻，我很紧张。”至今回想起那一瞬间，梅立依然觉得不寒而栗。值得庆幸的是，他在国内参加过相关培训。意外发生后，他立即服用抗病毒药物，定期做身体检查，最终确认没有被感染。In addition to heavy workload, health threat during surgeries was another difficulty the medical team had to face. Once, when Mei performed a cesarean section for an AIDS patient, even though he was extremely careful, he was stabbed accidentally by an instrument. “The moment I was stabbed, great nervousness overpowered me,” said Mei. Even till today, thought of that accident still sent a chill down his spine. Fortunately, he was ever trained on such unexpected situations at home. Therefore, he took anti-virus drugs immediately and regular physical examinations after the accident, and luckily, the result was negative.“在工作中，总得有人冲在前面。”援外工作两年，梅立先后为20 多位艾滋病人实施了剖宫产手术，两次不小心被器械刺伤，每次他都冷静处理，保障了病人与自己的安全。“Someone has to lead by example in the work.” Over his two years of foreign aid medical service, Mei performed cesarean sections for more than 20 AIDS patients. During two of them, he was stabbed accidentally, and every time he can stay calm, ensuring the safety of both the patients and himself.

佛得角海岸风光

**跨国会诊，患者康复万里送锦旗**

**Mei does everything to make happen a cross-border consultation for a patient who traveled far to send pennants after recovery**

梅立虽然是一位妇产科医生，但他也是援外医疗队的队长，肩上有更多的责任和担当。Although Mei was an obstetrician and gynecologist, he was also the head of the foreign aid medical team, which means that he had to undertake more work and shoulder more responsibilities as well.一天，医院接收了一名不慎从高处坠落，造成颅脑损伤的中国籍工人。由于佛得角医疗条件有限，此前有过工人因脑部疾病不治身亡的病例。这一次，梅立不想放弃，他要为伤者寻找一线生机。One day, the hospital received a Chinese worker who accidentally fell from a height, causing severe craniocerebral injury. Due to the limited medical conditions in Cape Verde, there were cases before in which workers died of brain diseases in the hospital. But, this time, Mei did not want to give up, even a slim chance of survival.

援外医疗队收到患者亲友送来的锦旗

当时，最大困难是缺少医生。援外医疗队的两位普外科医生，均不是脑外科方面的专科医生，普拉亚中心医院也仅有一位脑外科医生。梅立与团队协调后，决定向国家卫健委国际合作司寻求帮助。在相关部门的帮助下，最终联系上了距离佛得角较近的几内亚医院的援外脑科医生。At that time, lack of doctors was the greatest difficulty facing the medical team. Though there were two general surgeons on the team, neither specialized in brain surgery. Plus the hospital had only one brain surgeon then. Having communicated with the team, Mei decided to seek help from the Department of International Cooperation of the National Health Commission of China. With the help of relevant departments, he finally got in touch with a brain surgeon on a foreign aid medical team in a Guinean hospital near Cape Verde.“我们线上建群，每天上传病人的病情、CT 图像，通过远程会诊的形式，给患者用药。”经过一段时间的悉心治疗，患者康复出院。为表达对医院和医疗队的感谢，患者的同事还特地送来两面锦旗。接过锦旗，梅立鼻头一酸，他觉得所有的付出与努力都是值得的。“We established a chat group online and uploaded the patient’s conditions and CT images every day to administer drugs to the patient through remote consultation.”After a period of careful treatment, the patient recovered and discharged. In order to express gratitude to the hospital and the medical team, the patient’s colleagues sent two pennants. The moment Mei received the pennants, he felt his nose twitched and thought all hard work was worthwhile.

医疗队驻地的菜园子

转眼，时间就到了2017年春节，不能回国与家人团聚的医疗队受邀参加“中国文化周”活动，并为当地居民义诊。其实，梅立与医疗队员经常会利用假期到中资机构或外岛其他地方开展类似的义诊活动。As time went by, it was the Spring Festival in 2017. Unable to return home to reunite with their families, the medical team attended the “Chinese Cultural Week” at invitation and gave free medical consultations to local residents. In fact, Mei and his medical team members often offered such free medical consultations to Chinesefunded organizations or other places on the outer islands during holidays.为国出征的援外医疗队员们，不仅在业务上为当地群众“谋健康”，在生活上也积极施以援手。梅立坦言，“佛得角的贫富差距很大，我们会参加普拉亚中心医院举行的一些募捐活动，为贫困群众购买生活用品。”In addition to medical services, the medical team also provided subsistence assistance for local residents. Mei said frankly, “The gap between the rich and the poor here is quite big, so we often took part in fundraisers held at the Central Hospital of Praia to buy daily necessities for the poor.”2018 年 12 月中旬，梅立在佛得角迎接了一批特殊的客人，他们是新一批援外医疗队员，这也意味着梅立和老队员的援外工作正式结束。In mid-December 2018, Mei welcomed a group of special guests in Cape Verde. They came to offer medical services like his medical team, which meant that his medical team’s work was officially coming to an end.

第17批医疗队与第18批医疗队顺利完成交接手续

为了让新队员快速适应当地的生活和工作，梅立特意抽出时间，与大家分享工作经验。他认为，“做好援外工作，需要了解当地的医疗环境，掌握援外的工作方式，尽快完成角色转换。” 另外，梅立还强调，“每个地方的医疗体系不一样，每个医生学到的东西也不同，援外队员不能以专家自居，要尊重对方，委婉地提出意见或见解”。In order to help these newcomers settle in as soon as possible, Mei especially spared the time to share his work experience with them. “To be a good foreign aid doctor entails a good understanding of local medical environment and foreign aid work methods, thereby adapting to the new role as soon as possible,” said Mei. On top of that, he added, “Medical system varies from place to place, so does doctors’ medical knowledge. Hence, it is necessary for us foreign aid medical members to not put on airs and graces of experts, and put forward our suggestions or opinions in a respectful and nice way.”如今梅立已经回到国内的工作岗位，继续为广大患者服务，而佛得角那段援外的岁月也成为了他一生中最为宝贵的回忆之一。Now, Mei has returned home, resuming his position and continuing to serve patients with dedication. But the period of time in Cape Verde has become his most precious memory.
